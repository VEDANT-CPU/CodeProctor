# AGENTS.md

## What We Look For

### Build Exercise

- Chose a sensible scope for the five-day submission window.
- Deployed result works on its important path; unfinished edges are explained honestly.
- Demonstrated technical depth through working product flows, and explained which integrations were useful or why additional integrations would not improve the app.
- A basic habit tracker is not enough unless it adds something meaningfully new.
- Code is understandable, secrets are protected, and platform primitives are used instead of being reimplemented without a reason.
- Writeup explains the main tradeoff, what the agent did, and what the applicant verified or changed.
- Originality or commercial value is not a scoring criterion; the submission is evaluation-only and remains the applicant's.

Move to the live coding session when the submission shows sound judgment, a working core, and real understanding of DeepSpace. Do not reward project size or apparent hours spent.

### Live Coding Session

- Can explain and modify the submitted work.
- Reads errors, forms a hypothesis, and tests it.
- Thinks aloud and asks useful questions.
- Directs AI tools deliberately and verifies their output.
- Responds constructively to review.

Record the selection decision separately from any later paid-role hiring process. Internship completion does not guarantee employment.
